# Teaching note: the truncated axis

A bar chart encodes value as *length*, and length is only honest when every bar is measured from the same baseline — zero. Starting the axis at 77 stretches a 2-point difference into a bar three times taller than its neighbor, so the reader's eye concludes "Nursing dominates" while the numbers whisper "everyone is within two points." The fix is boring on purpose: start at zero, drop the shouting title, and annotate the actual range. Whenever a bar chart's axis doesn't start at zero, ask who benefits from the exaggeration.
